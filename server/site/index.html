A tiny bridge between your site and your terminal

Talk to your visitors
without another dashboard.

Add one script to your website. Visitors get a floating chat portal, and you answer directly from your terminal.

$ curl -fsSL https://peekaboo-477i.onrender.com/install.sh | sh
01

Install

Run the command above, then make the CLI available in your shell.

export PATH="$HOME/.local/bin:$PATH"
02

Create your site

Generate a unique site ID and save your private operator token.

peekaboo setup
03

Paste one tag

Add the printed snippet to your website. The chat button appears automatically.

<script data-site="...">
04

Start listening

Keep this running while visitors send messages from your website.

peekaboo listen
