Here's a complete breakdown of the **Peekaboo** project:

---

## What is Peekaboo?

**Peekaboo is a tiny chat widget that you drop onto any personal website. Visitors click one button, type a message, and it lands directly in your Telegram. You reply from Telegram, and your response shows back up on the visitor's screen — right on your website. No signup. No email hunting. No switching apps. No friction.**

---

## The Core Problem

People visit your portfolio, blog, or personal site. They like your work. They *want* to reach out. But the moment they hit friction:

| Friction Point | What Happens |
|----------------|--------------|
| **Contact forms** | Name, email, subject, message, captcha — feels like filling a tax return |
| **mailto: links** | Opens Apple Mail (not configured) or forces copy-paste to Gmail |
| **LinkedIn/WhatsApp links** | Forces them to leave your site and switch apps |
| **Enterprise chat widgets** | Heavy, slow, cookie banners, robotic "How can sales help you?" prompts |
| **Creating an account** | Just to say "nice work" — instant dealbreaker |

**The result:** Interested visitors quietly disappear. The gap between *"I like this person's work"* and *"I actually messaged them"* is where potential clients, collaborators, and fans vanish.

---

## How Peekaboo Works

### The Flow (3 Simple Steps)

```
Visitor on your site
        ↓
Clicks "talk to me" button
        ↓
Types message (no signup, no email)
        ↓
Message relays to YOUR Telegram
        ↓
You reply from Telegram (phone, watch, laptop)
        ↓
Your reply appears ON YOUR WEBSITE
        ↓
Visitor sees the conversation continue
```

### The Widget

- **One button** — "talk to me" or a small chat bubble
- **Text input** — just type and send
- **Threaded conversation** — messages appear in a chat interface
- **Live sync** — visitor's browser updates in real-time when you reply

### The Backend

- **Relay system** — connects your website to your preferred messaging platform
- **Currently supports:** Telegram
- **Coming soon:** Email, Slack, WhatsApp
- **No new dashboard** — you don't check another inbox

Peekaboo believes that:
1. **Conversations shouldn't require signup** — visitors are already on your site, let them talk
2. **You shouldn't learn new tools** — reply where you already live (Telegram, soon Slack/WhatsApp)
3. **Your website should be personal** — not a corporate contact center
4. **Friction is the enemy** — every extra click loses people
